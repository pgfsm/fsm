# Trademark policy

"pgfsm" and "fsm_core" are trademarks of the pgfsm project. The project's
licences (AGPL-3.0-only for the engine, Apache-2.0 for everything else) cover
the code, not these names. Neither licence grants rights to use them (see
Apache-2.0 §6 and AGPL-3.0 §7(e)).

This policy exists so that users can tell the project's own releases apart from
other people's versions.

## What you can do without asking

- **Refer to the project.** Say that your product works with, is compatible
  with, integrates with or is built on pgfsm or fsm_core, as long as you don't
  suggest that the project endorses or makes your product.
- **Redistribute unmodified releases** under their original names.
- **Write about the project:** articles, talks, tutorials, courses and books.
- **Name a fork while developing changes** you intend to contribute back, for
  example a personal GitHub fork.

## What needs a different name

- **Modified distributions and forks** you publish or offer to others, including
  hosted services running a modified engine. Choose a name that isn't
  confusingly similar to "pgfsm" or "fsm_core". You may say the product is
  "based on pgfsm".
- **Products, services, companies or domains** whose names include "pgfsm" or
  "fsm_core" in a way that suggests they come from the project.

## Questions

To ask about a use this policy doesn't cover, open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/pgfsm/fsm/issues), or use the contact in
[COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md).
